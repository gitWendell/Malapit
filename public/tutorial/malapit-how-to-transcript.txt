Malapit animated quick guide
Fictional demonstration. Synthetic narration. Direct payment is supported; online checkout and phone reminders are demo only. No real money is moved.

Find your kind of place
Looking for a place nearby? Start with the map. Tap a price, then open the home. Everything in this demo is fictional.

Have a place to rent out?
Have a place to rent out? Choose List your place. Add the details, location, photos and costs, then submit it for review.

Bring your existing rental
Already have a renter? Open My Rentals and add your room. Let's use eight thousand pesos a month. Check the rent and dates before saving.

Connect with your renter
Next, invite your renter using their Google sign in email. Check the details, create the invitation, and share the link yourself.

Now, the renter’s side
Here's the renter's side. Open the invitation, check the owner, rent and dates, then accept. You're now connected.

Create the monthly bill
Back with the owner, create a monthly rent bill. Check the covered dates, amount and due date. Both people can see this bill.

Set up future bills once
For an agreed calendar month cycle, you can turn on monthly bills. Review the first full month and rent before enabling. Pause them whenever you need. This does not deduct money.

Direct payment stays an option
You can still pay the owner directly by bank, wallet, or cash. After paying, choose Report a payment. Check the filled in details, change any partial amount, and add a new reference.

Reported is not yet confirmed
Here is a three thousand peso report. While it is awaiting confirmation, the recorded balance stays at eight thousand.

One place to check payments
Owners now check reports in one list. Compare the amount and reference with your original records, then confirm receipt. Three thousand confirmed, and five thousand left.

Cash works too
Already received the remaining five thousand in cash? Record it here and confirm you've received it. Now the bill is marked Paid.

Keep the full story
Open Payment history to see who reported and confirmed. Made a mistake? Use a correction and leave a reason. The original history stays.

A look at online checkout
Want to see the proposed online option? Open the payment demo. Tap the example reminder, choose a method, and try an approval. This is a fictional preview, not a connected payment service. No real payment or notification is sent.

Choose the way that works for you
Both people keep the same rent history. Direct payments remain available. Online checkout and phone reminders are still a demo. Nothing in this tutorial moves real money.
