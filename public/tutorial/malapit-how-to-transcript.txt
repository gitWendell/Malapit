Malapit animated quick guide
Fictional demonstration. Synthetic narration. Money is paid directly.

Find your kind of place
Looking for a place nearby? Start with the map. Tap a price, then open the home. Everything in this demo is fictional.

Have a place to rent out?
Have a place to rent out? Choose List your place. Add the details, location, photos and costs, then submit it for review.

Bring your existing rental
Already have a renter? Open My Rentals and add your room. Let's use eight thousand pesos a month. Check the rent and dates before saving.

Connect with your renter
Next, invite your renter using their Google sign in email. Check the details, create the invitation, and share the link yourself.

Now, the renter’s side
Here's the renter's side. Open the invitation, check the owner, rent and dates, then accept. You're now connected.

Create the monthly bill
Back with the owner, create a monthly rent bill. Check the covered dates, amount and due date. Both people can see this bill.

Paid the owner? Record it here.
Paid the owner directly? Report it here. Let's enter three thousand pesos, choose G Cash, and add the transaction reference. Then send the report.

A report still needs checking
Notice Awaiting confirmation? The balance is still eight thousand pesos. A report only reduces the balance after the owner checks and confirms it.

Check it. Then confirm.
The owner checks the actual bank or wallet record, then confirms receipt. There we go. Three thousand confirmed, and five thousand left.

Cash works too
Already received the remaining five thousand in cash? Record it here and confirm you've received it. Now the bill is marked Paid.

Keep the full story
Open Payment history to see who reported and confirmed. Made a mistake? Use a correction and leave a reason. The original history stays.

Both sides, on the same page
That's it. Find a rental, track your monthly bills, and keep both sides on the same page. Money is always paid directly to the owner.
