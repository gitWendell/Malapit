Malapit quick guide
Fictional demonstration data. No money transferred.

Find a nearby rental
Welcome to Malapit. Use the map, location search and filters to find nearby rentals. The homes and people shown in this guide are fictional.

Advertise a property
To advertise a property, choose List your place. Add its details, location, photos and costs. A listing goes through review before appearing publicly.

Add a private unit
For an existing rental, open My Rentals and add your unit or room. Record the rent, due day and dates agreed with your renter. This does not publish a listing.

Invite your renter
Invite the renter using their Google sign in email. Check the unit details first. Copy and share the invitation link yourself. Malapit does not send an email.

Renter reviews the invitation
In the renter account, review the invitation. Check the owner, address, rent and rental dates before accepting. This connects the record; it does not sign a lease.

Create the monthly bill
The owner can now create a rent bill. Check its billing month, covered dates, amount and due date. Enter the agreed amount for a partial month yourself.

Both accounts see the bill
The renter sees the same bill in My Rentals. Open it to review the amount, covered period and remaining balance. Money is paid directly to the owner.

Report a payment
After paying the owner directly, choose Report a payment. Enter the amount, method, date and transaction reference. You can report a partial payment.

Wait for owner confirmation
The report is now awaiting confirmation. Its amount is shown separately. Reporting a payment does not mark the bill paid or reduce the confirmed balance.

Owner checks receipt
The owner opens the report and checks the original cash, bank or wallet records. Confirm receipt only after checking. If something is wrong, request a correction with a reason.

See the remaining balance
Once confirmed by the owner, the payment reduces the balance. In this example, three thousand pesos is confirmed and five thousand pesos remains.

Record money already received
Owners can record money they have already received, including cash. Check the amount and date, choose the method, and confirm receipt before saving.

Paid in the records
The bill shows Paid when the owner confirmed records cover the full amount. This is a shared record of the owner's confirmation, not a transfer processed by Malapit.

Keep the payment history
Open Payment history to review who reported, confirmed or corrected a record. The original amounts and decisions remain visible to the original owner and renter.

Correct a confirmed record
To correct a confirmed record, the owner uses a reversal and gives a reason. A reversal changes the recorded balance. It does not move or return money.

Your records, in one place
Your rental map, bills and owner confirmed payment records are in one Malapit account. Use the monthly overview to stay organised. Always arrange actual money transfers directly.
