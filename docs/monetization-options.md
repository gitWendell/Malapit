# Malapit revenue proposal

November 18, 2026. Recommendation for review; no prices, subscriptions, advertising sales or payment fees have been activated. This updates the revenue recommendation in the earlier business process as private rental management has grown. It does not change the current free test or its limits.

## Main recommendation

Keep renter search, basic listings and the direct-to-owner payment option free. Make the first paid offer an optional management plan for owners with several units. Its value should come from saving administrative work even when an owner receives rent outside Malapit. A charge on online checkout alone would earn nothing from direct payments and depends on provider approval and actual usage.

Start with one proposed price to test: **PHP 199 per owner per month, covering up to 10 private units**. This is a hypothesis, not a researched Philippine market price or an approved commercial offer. Monthly owner reports and CSV downloads have now been added to the free test so their usefulness can be assessed. Carefully scoped staff access remains a possible later addition. A future paid offer needs additional agreed value; adding reports to the free test does not authorize charging for them. Agree plan, support, cancellation and billing terms before selling anything. Do not remove current free-test features or silently reduce existing limits.

Ten paying owners at PHP 199 would produce **PHP 1,990 monthly gross revenue**. Subtract hosting, storage and delivery, payment collection costs, taxes, refunds and support time to assess profit. Ten owners with ten units each already use the present 100-unit test capacity; more customers or larger portfolios require a capacity and cost review. This small example is a validation target, not a full-income forecast.

The useful early signals are owners returning each month, completing bills and receipt checks with less work, and choosing to renew a paid offer after receiving the promised features. Online competitor research cannot establish local willingness to pay PHP 199.

## Later income

Once an area has real renter traffic, test optional **PHP 99 featured placement for seven days** on an approved listing. This is another unvalidated example price, not a current offer. Label it "Sponsored", keep it relevant to the renter's filters and show the exact placement, period, delivery report and refund terms. Do not promise views, inquiries or a tenant. Payment must never buy approval or an ownership-verification claim.

An online payment convenience or platform fee is a later option only after an approved provider arrangement. Provider processing fees are costs, not automatically Malapit's revenue. Show the full amount and who pays each fee before authorization. Keep owner rent payouts separate from Malapit service income. Direct-to-owner payments stay available and need the owner's receipt check. The current browser checkout demo earns nothing and makes no payment.

## Evidence and its limits

- [Landlord Studio's official free-plan guide](https://help.landlordstudio.com/en/articles/8389595-landlord-studio-go-free-plan-limits-and-what-s-included) describes a free tier and paid management plans with additional reports and integrations. This supports the general owner-subscription model; it is not evidence for Malapit's Philippine price or demand.
- [Rentpad's free property posting page](https://rentpad.com.ph/post-property-for-rent) and [official featured-property instructions](https://rentpad.com.ph/content/how-do-i-feature-a-property/37410b3849) show free discovery alongside paid promotion in a Philippine rental service. Their package terms and prices are not Malapit's, and no comparable price is assumed here.
- [PayMongo linked-account prerequisites](https://docs.paymongo.com/docs/account-settings-linked-accounts) require an eligible platform arrangement and approval. See [payment readiness](payment-checkout-readiness.md) before integrating provider test mode or enabling any live collection.

## Next product decision

Test the monthly owner report and downloadable bill/payment history in the free release with a few owners over a rent cycle. Use what actually saves work to define a future optional management plan. Do not build subscription checkout or publish pricing until the offer, operating requirements and delivery costs are ready.
