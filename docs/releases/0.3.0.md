# Malapit 0.3.0 — Viewing requests and clear next steps

Released November 27, 2026. Public beta: https://malapit.pages.dev/.

Each real inquiry now shows the next action for its owner and renter: arrange an optional viewing, review rental details, accept the rental, open its bill, and check payment status. The bill link prioritizes reports awaiting review, then the earliest unpaid bill. A paid later bill cannot hide an earlier balance. A paid status applies to the selected bill, not all future rent.

## Viewing requests

- Either person can suggest a date, Philippine time, 30- or 60-minute duration, and private meeting note. The recipient confirms or declines. Either person can cancel or suggest another time.
- Rescheduling cancels the earlier slot and creates a fresh proposal in one D1 transaction. The new time needs confirmation. Earlier appointments remain in history, with a separate immutable action log.
- Confirmed appointments cannot overlap for either person, including when someone is an owner in one conversation and a renter in another. Adjacent appointments are allowed.
- Only the original owner and renter can access viewing details. Review permissions do not grant access. Forms retain the original appointment version; a change by the other person requires a refresh before another change.
- The owner can mark a confirmed viewing complete after its start time. Marking a listing rented or withdrawn cancels outstanding viewings and alerts their renters.
- Viewing updates appear in the existing bell. Browser push remains optional, uses general text, and opens the correct conversation. No email or SMS service was added.

Viewings are optional and do not reserve a property, sign a lease, create a bill or charge anyone. Owners can send agreed rental details directly. Renter acceptance still connects My Rentals, creates the agreed first bill and marks the public listing rented.

The free beta allows 20 proposals per person per Philippine day and 5,000 stored proposals. History shows the latest 20. Appointment notes are not public and are excluded from notification text. The privacy notice and written guide now describe this flow; the earlier tutorial video has not been rerecorded.

- TypeScript, the production build and 172 unit tests passed.
- 95 isolated built-Worker API checks covered private access, recipient confirmation, duplicate retries, overlapping appointments, competing reschedules, atomic rollback, notifications, rental acceptance and payment progress across multiple bills.
- 50 Chrome checks covered phone and desktop layouts, both account roles, viewing history, cancellation, stale forms, skipping a viewing, acceptance, bill links, payment reporting and owner confirmation. A separate 32-check Chrome regression also verified the path that skips a viewing. Test data and photo storage stayed in disposable local databases; no real rental, transfer or browser push was created.
- Migration `0013_inquiry_viewings.sql` adds viewing and action-history tables, indexes and database guards. Apply it before deploying the matching Worker. Existing account, listing, rental and payment records stay in place.
- The public Pages entry continues forwarding to the existing `malapit` Worker and the same D1 and Supabase project. No new provider, subscription, credential or paid plan was added.

Renters still pay the owner through the owner's receiving details. Malapit records the owner's confirmation; it does not collect, hold, transfer or automatically verify money. Physical Android installation and real device push delivery still need device verification.
